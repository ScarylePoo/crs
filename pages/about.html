How we got here

We started as the people other IT companies called

For six years we were the subcontractor. When a provider had a client with ransomware on a Saturday, a mail server that had quietly been relaying spam for a month, or a departed administrator who still had the keys, we were the number they rang.

After enough of those weekends the pattern was hard to ignore. Almost none of it was sophisticated. It was an unpatched firewall, a shared password, a backup nobody had ever restored. The expensive emergency was nearly always a cheap chore that had been skipped.

So we stopped cleaning up after other people's clients and took on our own. We do the boring chores on schedule, we watch for the things that still get through, and when something does go wrong the person who calls you is someone you have already met.
