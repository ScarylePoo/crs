The first seventy-two hours

Every incident is different in its details and the same in its shape. This is the order we work in, and roughly how long each part takes for an office of twenty to fifty people.

  1. Hour 0 to 2

    Contain

    Isolate affected machines, disable compromised accounts, block the attacker's route out. Nothing is wiped. Evidence is worth more than tidiness at this point.

  2. Hour 2 to 24

    Understand

    How they got in, how long they were there, what they touched. This decides whether you have a notification duty, and it is the first thing your insurer will ask.

  3. Day 1 to 3

    Rebuild

    Clean machines from known-good images, data restored from backups we have verified predate the intrusion, every credential rotated. Staff back at work in priority order.

  4. Week 2

    Report

    A written account: timeline, root cause, what was and was not accessed, and the specific changes that would have prevented it.
