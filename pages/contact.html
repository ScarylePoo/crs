The assessment is an hour and it is free

It is a conversation, not a pitch. About a third of the time we tell people they are fine as they are.

  1. You tell us the shape of things. How many people, how many sites, what you run, who looks after it today.
  2. We ask what worries you. Usually it is one specific thing, and usually it is the right thing to worry about.
  3. We look from the outside. With your permission, a quick check of what your organization exposes to the internet.
  4. You get a short written note. What we saw, what we would do, and what it would cost. Yours to keep either way.
