The small print, in large print

Questions about money

Why per site and not per user?

Because the work is per site. A firewall, a network and a set of backups cost the same to look after whether twelve or fifteen people sit behind them. Per-user pricing mostly punishes you for hiring.

Is incident recovery really unlimited?

For incidents on systems we manage, yes. If you ask us to recover something we were never given access to, we will quote a day rate before starting.

What does it cost to leave?

Nothing. Thirty days notice, and we hand over documentation, credentials and configuration to whoever comes next. We would rather be easy to leave and kept anyway.

Is there an onboarding fee?

One month's rate, once. It covers the first audit and the cleanup that nearly always follows it. Here is what onboarding involves.

Not sure which one you are?

Tell us how many people and how many buildings. We will tell you the number on the first call.
